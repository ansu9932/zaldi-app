This Delivery Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is between **Moolya India Private
Limited**, operator of the **next** platform (the "Company"), and the individual
delivery partner who registers on the next Rider app (the "Rider").

By accepting this Agreement during onboarding, the Rider agrees to the following.

---

## 1. Independent contractor relationship

1.1 The Rider is an **independent contractor** and not an employee, agent, or worker
of the Company. The Rider is free to accept or decline delivery offers and to provide
services to others.

1.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship. The Rider is
responsible for their own taxes, vehicle, insurance, licence, and statutory dues.

---

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 The Rider must be **18+**, hold a valid driving licence and vehicle registration
(where a motor vehicle is used), and provide valid ID and bank details.

2.2 The Rider must keep their documents valid and provide updates on request.

---

## 3. Providing services

3.1 When online, the Rider may receive delivery offers. The Platform offers each order
to the **nearest available Rider first**; the Rider may accept or decline.

3.2 On accepting, the Rider agrees to collect the order from the Merchant, verify
items, and deliver promptly and safely to the customer, sharing live location during
the active delivery.

3.3 For age-restricted items, the Rider must verify the customer's age and may refuse
handover if verification fails.

3.4 The Rider must behave courteously and lawfully and must not tamper with, consume,
or open customer orders.

---

## 4. Payments to the Rider

4.1 For each completed delivery, the Rider earns a **base payout of ₹[18]** plus
**₹[6] per kilometre** of the delivery distance (the "Delivery Fee"), as shown in the
app at the time of the offer.

4.2 **Tips** are paid **100%** to the Rider.

4.3 Payouts are settled to the Rider's registered bank account on a **[weekly]** cycle,
subject to reconciliation and deduction of any amounts owed (e.g. cash-on-delivery
collected on the Company's/Merchant's behalf).

4.4 Payout rates may change on **[15] days'** notice via the app.

---

## 5. Cash handling

5.1 For cash-on-delivery orders, the Rider collects cash on behalf of the Company/
Merchant and must remit/reconcile it as instructed. Unremitted cash may be adjusted
against payouts.

---

## 6. Conduct, safety & compliance

6.1 The Rider must obey all traffic and safety laws, wear a helmet where applicable,
and not drive under the influence.

6.2 The Rider must not engage in fraud (e.g. fake deliveries, GPS spoofing, fake
acceptances) or any abusive/illegal behaviour. Such conduct may lead to immediate
deactivation and legal action.

---

## 7. Account deactivation & term

7.1 Either party may end this Agreement at any time. The Company may suspend or
deactivate the Rider for fraud, safety violations, repeated cancellations, or breach.

7.2 Earned payouts for completed deliveries survive termination.

---

## 8. Liability & indemnity

8.1 The Rider is responsible for their own acts, vehicle, and safety, and indemnifies
the Company against claims arising from the Rider's negligence, breach, or unlawful
conduct.

8.2 The Company's liability to the Rider is limited to unpaid, earned payouts.

---

## 9. Data

The Rider's location and activity are processed to operate the service per the
[Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Customer details shared with the Rider must be
used only to complete the delivery and then not retained or misused.

---

## 10. Governing law

Governed by the laws of India; disputes subject to courts/arbitration at
**[CITY/STATE]**.
