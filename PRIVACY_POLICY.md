## 2. Information we collect

### a) Information you give us
- **Account & contact details:** your name and phone number.
- **Delivery details:** your delivery address(es) and the location pin you set.
- **Order information:** items ordered, order history, tips, and notes.

### b) Information collected automatically
- **Location data:** with your permission, we use your device's location to (i)
  check whether delivery is available in your area and (ii) show live order
  tracking. The customer app uses location only **while the app is in use**. The
  rider app uses location while a delivery is active to share the rider's live
  position with the customer.
- **Device & notification token:** a push-notification token so we can send you
  order updates.
- **Usage/diagnostic data:** basic technical logs to keep the service working.

### c) Payment information
- Online payments are processed by **Razorpay**. We do **not** store your full card
  or UPI credentials on our servers; that data is handled by Razorpay under their
  own privacy policy. We only receive a payment status (success/failure) and a
  reference id.

We do **not** knowingly collect data from children under 13. Some products (e.g.
alcohol/tobacco where applicable) require you to confirm you are 18+.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and deliver your orders, and route them to the nearest store and rider.
- To show live order tracking and send order-status notifications.
- To process payments and prevent fraud.
- To provide customer support.
- To improve and secure the service.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary data with:
- **The merchant/store** fulfilling your order (your name, items, and area).
- **The delivery rider** (your name, phone, and delivery address) to complete the
  delivery.
- **Service providers** that run the Platform on our behalf:
  - **Supabase** — secure database and backend hosting.
  - **Razorpay** — payment processing.
  - **Expo / Google (FCM) & Apple (APNs)** — push notifications and app updates.
  - **Google Maps** and/or **OpenStreetMap** — maps and routing.
  - **OpenWeather** — weather (used for delivery-condition fees).

These providers may process data outside your state under their own policies and
appropriate safeguards.
