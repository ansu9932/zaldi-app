This Merchant Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

- **Moolya India Private Limited**, operator of the **next** platform, registered
  office at **[REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS]**, CIN **[CIN]** (the "Company"); and
- the store/business onboarding onto the Platform (the "Merchant"), whose details are
  captured at onboarding.

By accepting this Agreement (electronically during onboarding or by signature), the
Merchant agrees to the following.

---

## 1. Scope

1.1 The Company operates a technology marketplace ("Platform") that lists the
Merchant's products and routes customer orders to the Merchant for fulfilment, and
arranges last-mile delivery via independent Riders.

1.2 The Merchant is an **independent business**. Nothing here creates a partnership,
employment, agency (except limited payment-collection agency as described), or joint
venture between the parties.

---

## 2. Merchant obligations

2.1 List accurate product names, prices, units, images and stock status, and keep
them updated.

2.2 Sell only genuine, lawful products that the Merchant is licensed/authorised to
sell (including any required FSSAI, drug, or other licences). The Merchant is solely
responsible for product quality, safety, packaging, labelling, expiry, and legal
compliance.

2.3 Accept and prepare orders promptly during stated operating hours, mark them
"ready" accurately, and hand them to the assigned Rider with the correct items.

2.4 Honour the price shown to the customer at order time.

2.5 Maintain hygiene and statutory standards and provide valid tax invoices where
required.

---

## 3. Commission & payments ("everyone wins" model)

3.1 In consideration for the Platform's services, the Company retains a **commission
of [12]% of the item subtotal** of each successfully delivered order (the "Commission
Rate"). The Merchant therefore receives **[88]%** of the item subtotal.

3.2 The Commission Rate may be revised on **[30] days'** prior written/in-app notice.

3.3 The Company (or its payment partner) may **collect payment from customers on the
Merchant's behalf** and remit the Merchant's share after deducting the Commission and
any applicable taxes/chargebacks/refunds.

3.4 **Settlement cycle:** Merchant payouts are remitted **[weekly]** to the Merchant's
registered bank account, subject to reconciliation.

3.5 Taxes (GST etc.) are the responsibility of the respective party as per law. The
Company will issue commission invoices as required.

---

## 4. Refunds, cancellations & quality issues

4.1 Where a customer complaint about wrong/damaged/expired/poor-quality items is
upheld, the cost of the refund/replacement may be **borne by the Merchant**, and the
Company may adjust it against payouts.

4.2 Repeated quality failures, fake stock, or order rejections may lead to reduced
order routing or suspension.

---

## 5. Brand & data

5.1 The Merchant grants the Company a licence to display the Merchant's name, logo and
product details on the Platform for the purpose of operating the service.

5.2 Each party will protect customer and business data and use it only to operate the
service, in line with the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and applicable law.

---

## 6. Term & termination

6.1 This Agreement is effective from onboarding and continues until terminated.

6.2 Either party may terminate on **[15] days'** notice. The Company may suspend or
terminate immediately for fraud, illegal products, safety risks, or material breach.

6.3 Outstanding settlements due to the Merchant for delivered orders survive
termination.

---

## 7. Liability & indemnity

7.1 The Merchant is liable for, and indemnifies the Company against, claims arising
from its products (quality, safety, legality), its staff, and its breaches.

7.2 The Company's aggregate liability to the Merchant for any claim is limited to the
Commission earned by the Company from that Merchant in the **[preceding 1 month]**.

---
